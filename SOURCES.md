## Each source in full

### `fda.additives.notgras`

**FDA Office of Food Additive Safety** (first party) · monthly · active

Substances FDA has determined are NOT GRAS after the fact — the removal end of the gate, and the outcome side of any revocation petition. 21 substances at first capture, including Amanita muscaria, androstenedione and areca nut.
Small and slow-growing. Its value is the timestamp: an addition here is a significant regulatory event that the publisher records without a date, so the archive supplies the one thing the source does not.

### `fda.additives.petitions`

**FDA Office of Food Additive Safety** (first party) · monthly · active

A queue of petitions asking FDA to permit, amend or revoke a food or colour additive. Captures dwell time, the flip between "under review" and "held in abeyance", and — by absence in a later capture — which petitions quietly vanish without a matching final rule.
Petitioners are overwhelmingly companies and trade bodies; at the 2026-08-19 snapshot one of 32 was a private citizen filing a citizen petition. Names appear only as parties to a public federal proceeding, published by FDA as an inseparable part of it, with no contact details. The derived tables carry no petitioner column: every question this source answers — dwell time, status transitions, disappearance — uses the petition number, and deleting the names leaves the dataset intact.

- <https://www.cfsanappsexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/?set=FAP-CAP>

### `fda.animalfeed.consultations`

**FDA Center for Veterinary Medicine** (first party) · monthly · active

The animal-feed half of the gate. Firms consult FDA voluntarily about new feed ingredients while the formal FAP and GRAS routes are under review. Nine consultations at first capture (AFIC 0001-0009), eight pending and one complete.
Thin on its own. Captured because the programme is explicitly interim, so the record of what was consulted during the interim plausibly disappears with the programme rather than being migrated anywhere.

## Reusing this data

The captures in this repository are bytes as the publisher served them. Each
source's own licence or terms of service govern reuse — they are listed per
source above and they are not all the same. This repository's code is separate
from the data it collects; check the source before redistributing.
